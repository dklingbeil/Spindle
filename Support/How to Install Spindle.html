How to install Spindle

1. Drag Spindle onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Spindle.
macOS will say that it could not verify Spindle. That is expected: Spindle isn't registered with Apple, so macOS can't check it for you. Click Done (on older versions of macOS, Cancel). Do not choose Move to Trash.

3. Tell macOS you trust it.
Open the Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security, find the line about Spindle, and click Open Anyway. Click Open Anyway once more when asked, and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

4. That's it.
Spindle opens and shows a short introduction. From now on it opens like any other app; you only do this once.

Where did it go? Spindle has no window and no icon in the Dock. It draws on your desktop, behind your windows, and its settings are under the record icon in the menu bar at the top right of the screen.

To remove it, choose Quit Spindle from that menu and drag Spindle from Applications to the Trash.
